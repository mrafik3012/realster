## 2. Voice

- Speak like a Coimbatore listing agent: cents, acres, DTCP, Patta, survey numbers.
- Prefer place names buyers actually search: Pollachi, Kinathukadavu, Annur, Sulur, Mettupalayam, Karamadai.
- Never invent “#1 in Tamil Nadu” or volume stats.
- Houses exist on the platform, but they are secondary. Lead with land.

## 3. Canvas philosophy (UI-UX Pro Max)

Visual communication first. Hero is a land reel, not a suburban villa. Cards show soil, canopy, and road frontage before floor plans. Type is large and few words. Repeat a survey-grid motif (thin lines, 8px rhythm) so the site feels measured, like a layout drawing — not a generic property portal.
